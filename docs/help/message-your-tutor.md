# Message your tutor

For: Students, Parents · Topic: Messages and notes

Open Messages. There’s one conversation for each student and tutor.

Also searched as: chat, contact, text, talk, email tutor, conversation.

1. Open **Messages** and choose the conversation with your tutor.
2. Write your message and send it. Your tutor is told there’s a new message.

## From your child’s page (for Parents)

On your **Family** page, open your child. The Message button with the tutor’s name opens the same conversation.

## Who can read it

Parents and the Learn2Lead team can read every conversation. It keeps students and tutors safe, so keep all contact there.
