# Find class notes and homework

For: Students, Parents · Topic: Messages and notes

After each class your tutor writes what you covered and any homework.

Also searched as: notes, homework, summary, history, past classes.

## On your home page (for Students)

- The **Homework** card lists homework from your recent classes.
- **Class history** has every class with your tutor’s notes.

## On your child’s page (for Parents)

On your **Family** page, open your child. **Class history** has each class with the tutor’s notes and homework.

## By email

A **Class Completed** email arrives after each class with the notes and the homework.
