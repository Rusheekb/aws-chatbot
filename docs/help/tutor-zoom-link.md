# Where do I set my meeting link?

For: Tutors · Topic: Classes

In your settings. Every class needs one, and it fills in when you schedule.

Also searched as: zoom, google meet, meet, link, meeting, settings, add, save, change, required, video.

Select your name at the bottom left (on a phone, your initials at the top right), then choose **Settings**. Under **Default meeting link**, paste your Zoom or Google Meet link and choose **Save**. It fills in on every class you schedule from then on; classes you already scheduled keep their link (change one with **Edit class**).
