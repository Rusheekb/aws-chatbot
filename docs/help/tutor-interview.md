# When is my interview?

For: Tutors · Topic: Your account

Once it’s booked, it’s at the top of your Schedule.

Also searched as: interview, call, zoom, meeting, reschedule.

Once we’ve agreed a time with you by email, we book your 30-minute interview. It shows at the top of your **Schedule** with a **Join interview** button, and we email you the details and a calendar invite. Need a different time? Email learn2leadtutoring@gmail.com.
