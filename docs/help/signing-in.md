# Signing in

For: Students, Parents, Tutors · Topic: Your account

Use your email or username and your password.

1. Go to learn2lead.page and choose **Sign in**.
2. Enter your **Email or username** and your **Password**.

## Forgot your password?

Choose **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and we’ll email you a link to set a new one.

## If your parent set up your account (for Students)

Your parent sets up your sign-in when they add you. Use the username and temporary password they give you; you’ll choose your own password the first time.

## Your child’s sign-in (for Parents)

Each child gets their own username and temporary password when you add them. Write the details down or use **Copy** for the password; your child chooses their own password the first time they sign in, and never sees prices or payments. Forgot it? On your **Family** page, open your child and choose **Reset password** under **Sign-in**.

## Still can’t get in?

Email learn2leadtutoring@gmail.com and we’ll sort it out.
