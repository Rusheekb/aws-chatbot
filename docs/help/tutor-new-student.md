# Get a new student

For: Tutors · Topic: Classes

When we have a student who fits, we talk it over with you and the family first.

1. When a family asks for a tutor and you’re a good fit, we get in touch to talk it over with you and the family.
2. Once everyone agrees, we pair you, and we email you about your new student.
3. They show under **Students**, and you can schedule your first class.
