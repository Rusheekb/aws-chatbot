# Put classes in your calendar

For: Students, Parents, Tutors · Topic: Classes

Subscribe once and your classes appear in Google, Apple or Outlook Calendar.

Also searched as: google, apple, outlook, ical, calendar, sync.

1. To open your settings, select your name at the bottom left (on a phone, your initials at the top right), then choose **Settings**.
2. Under **Calendar sync**, choose **Add to Google Calendar** or **Add to Apple Calendar**.
3. Your calendar app picks up new and changed classes on its own, which can take a few hours.

## From your schedule (for Tutors)

The **Calendar sync** button on your **Schedule** does the same.

## Keep the link private

Anyone who has your calendar link can see your class schedule. If it got shared by mistake, reset it in the same place.
