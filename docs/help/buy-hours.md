# Buy hours

For: Students, Parents · Topic: Hours and payments

Hours come in 4, 8 and 12-hour packs, and they never expire.

Also searched as: buy, pay, price, pack, credits, purchase, cost.

Only the person who pays for the account can do this. If a parent pays for a student, the parent manages it.

1. Go to Pricing. The hours card on your home page links there too.
2. Choose **One-time** to pay once, or **Auto-refill** to buy the same pack again whenever you run out of hours.
3. Pick a pack and pay by card. The hours are added as soon as the payment goes through.

## How hours work

- Each class uses as many hours as it lasts, in half-hour steps: a 1-hour class uses 1 hour.
- Hours never expire, and there’s no subscription to cancel.

## For families (for Parents)

You buy hours once for the whole family. Every child’s classes use the family’s hours.
