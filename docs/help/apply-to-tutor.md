# Apply to tutor

For: Tutors · Topic: Your account

A short application, about 5 minutes. It saves as you go.

Also searched as: apply, application, interview, join, become a tutor.

1. Sign up, then open **Apply to tutor** from your dashboard.
2. Work through the six short steps. Your answers save as you go, so you can finish later.
3. Agree to the platform rules and send it. We’ll email you to set up a short, 30-minute interview.
