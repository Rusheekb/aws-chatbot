# Assigning AI practice

For: Tutors · Topic: Classes

Multiple-choice questions drafted from your class notes. You check every one before your student sees it.

For math and science classes, AI drafts multiple-choice questions from your notes for a class you’ve logged. Nothing reaches your student until you’ve read every question and assigned it. It isn’t available for students under 13, without an age on file, or whose family has turned AI practice off.

## Assign practice

1. When you log a class with **Mark complete**, tick **Assign practice after saving**. For a class you’ve already logged, find it in **History** on your **Schedule** and choose **Assign practice**.
2. Under **What should the questions focus on?**, add anything to aim for if you like, and choose how hard. Then choose **Draft practice**.
3. Read every question, with its answer, hint and worked solution: your student sees all of them. Edit or remove anything you want to change, or choose **New draft** for a fresh set. Each class gets up to 3 drafts.
4. Pick a **Due** date (a week away unless you change it), tick that you’ve read every question, then assign it.

## What the AI sees

- To draft the questions, Learn2Lead sends Anthropic’s Claude this class’s notes and homework, notes from recent classes in the same subject, the student’s grade, level, goal, course and interests, and your focus.
- Your name and your student’s are removed first. Other names aren’t, so don’t write other people’s names or anything private in your notes.
- Anthropic doesn’t use it to train its models.

## After you assign it

- It shows on your student’s home page, and they’re emailed (their parent too, if they have one).
- If it isn’t finished, they get one reminder before it’s due. It stays open after the due date.
- Choose **Practice assigned** on the class to see each answer your student chose, and which were right.
- To change it, choose **Take it back**. Your student (and their parent) stop seeing it, and you can draft again while the class has drafts left.
- Students and parents can **Report a problem** with any question. It goes to the Learn2Lead team, who may put the practice on hold while they check it, or correct a marked answer and tell you. Answers are then marked against the corrected one.
