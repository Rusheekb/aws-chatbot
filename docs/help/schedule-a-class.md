# Schedule a class

For: Tutors · Topic: Classes

Schedule → Schedule a class. Turn on Repeat weekly for a regular time.

Also searched as: book, add, create, new class, session, recurring, weekly, calendar.

1. Open **Schedule** and choose **Schedule a class**.
2. Pick the **Student** and **Subject**, and give the class a **Title**.
3. Choose the **Date**, **Start time** and **End time** in your own time zone. If your student is in a different one, the form shows their time too.
4. Check the **Meeting link**. It fills in from your settings. If it’s empty, paste your Zoom or Google Meet link (every class needs one) and leave **Save this link for next time** ticked.
5. For a regular time each week, turn on **Repeat weekly** and choose **Repeat until** (up to 12 weeks).
6. Choose **Schedule class**. The class shows on the family’s schedule straight away, and they get a reminder email the day before.

## Changing or cancelling

- Open the class on your schedule and choose **Edit class**, **Delete class** (just that one) or **Delete all recurring** (the whole weekly series).
- Let the family know in **Messages** whenever you change or cancel a class.

## Good to know

- You can’t schedule a class for a student with no hours left; the form tells you when that happens. Let the family know, or email learn2leadtutoring@gmail.com.
- Save your meeting link once in your settings (select your name at the bottom left (on a phone, your initials at the top right), then **Settings**) and it fills in on every class you schedule.
