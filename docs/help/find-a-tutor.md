# Ask for a tutor

For: Students, Parents · Topic: Classes

Tell us what you need in about 3 minutes, and we’ll pick a tutor who fits.

## Asking for your child (for Parents)

1. On your **Family** page, open your child.
2. Choose **Find a tutor**, or **Tutor for another subject** if they already have one.

## Asking for yourself (for Students)

Open Find a tutor. It’s also on your **Home** page while you’re getting started.

## Then

1. Answer four short steps about the subject, your goals and when you’re free.
2. We pick a tutor who fits and email you once you’re paired. Their classes then show on your schedule.
