# Add a child

For: Parents · Topic: Family

Family → Add a student. Your child doesn’t need an email.

Also searched as: child, kid, son, daughter, student, family.

1. On your **Family** page, choose **Add a student**.
2. Enter their name and grade. An email is optional; without one, class emails come to you.
3. We fill in a username and temporary password for them. Pass these on; they choose their own password the first time.
4. Open their page and choose **Find a tutor** to tell us what they need.

- Hours are shared by the whole family.
- Forgot their password? You can reset it from their page. See Signing in.
