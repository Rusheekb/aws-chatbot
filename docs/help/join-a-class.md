# Join your class

For: Students, Parents · Topic: Classes

Your class link is at the top of your home page. Press Join class when it’s time.

## From your home page (for Students)

1. Sign in a few minutes before class. Your **Home** page opens with your next class at the top.
2. Press **Join class**. The meeting (Zoom or Google Meet) opens in a new tab, and you can join from your browser or the app.
3. Before class the card shows when it **Starts**. Once class begins it shows **Live now**.

## From your Family page (for Parents)

1. Sign in a few minutes before class. Your **Family** page shows your children’s next class at the top.
2. Press **Join class** on the device your child uses for class. The meeting opens in a new tab.
3. If your child has their own sign-in, they can join from their own home page the same way.

## Other ways in

- On **Schedule**, classes happening today have a **Join** button. For any class, tap it and choose **Join class**.
- The reminder email the day before has a **Join class** button.
- In a class’s details, **Add to Calendar** puts the class, with its link, in your own calendar.

## No Join button?

If the card says **Your tutor will add the meeting link.**, the link isn’t set yet. Send your tutor a note in **Messages**, or email learn2leadtutoring@gmail.com.
