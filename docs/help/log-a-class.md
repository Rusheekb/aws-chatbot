# Log a class

For: Tutors · Topic: Classes

After every class, the same day: Schedule → Ready to log → Mark complete.

Also searched as: complete, finish, notes, homework, hours, record, mark complete, paid.

1. When a class ends, it shows under **Ready to log** on your **Schedule**.
2. Choose **Mark complete**.
3. Check **Hours taught**. It starts at the scheduled length; change it if the class ran longer or shorter, in half-hour steps.
4. Write **What was covered in this class?** and any **Homework/Follow-up tasks assigned**.
5. Tick the confirmation box and choose **Complete & save**.

## What happens next

- The family gets a **Class Completed** email with your notes, the homework and the hours they have left.
- The hours come off the student’s balance. Logged hours are what you’re paid for, every two weeks.
- The class moves to **History** on your **Schedule**.

## If it won’t log

- A message saying the student is out of hours means nothing was charged. Choose **Copy details** and email them to learn2leadtutoring@gmail.com. You can log the class once the family adds hours.
- Logging can’t be undone, so check the hours before you save. If something’s wrong, email us.
- If a student didn’t show up, message the family and tell the Learn2Lead team before logging anything.
