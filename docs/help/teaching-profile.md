# Update your teaching profile

For: Tutors · Topic: Your account

In your profile: who you teach and when you’re free.

Also searched as: availability, subjects, grades, hours, schedule.

- Select your name at the bottom left (on a phone, your initials at the top right), then choose **Teaching**.
- Keep **When you can teach** up to date. We use it to match students with you.
- If you change a subject’s level or your experience with learning differences, our team checks it again before it counts for matching.
