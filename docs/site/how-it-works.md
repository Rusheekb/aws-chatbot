# How Learn2Lead works

Getting started takes three steps.

## 1. Choose your hours

Buy a 4, 8 or 12-hour pack. Pay once, or turn on auto-refill so you never run low.

## 2. Meet your tutor

Learn2Lead pairs each student with a tutor based on their subject, goals, learning style and schedule, and lets you know by email once you are matched. Not sure where to start? Tell Learn2Lead what you need and they'll find the right tutor.

## 3. Learn live, online

All sessions are online video classes, so students can learn from anywhere. Each session is one-on-one with a dedicated tutor who focuses entirely on the student. Join each class with one tap. Your tutor books classes at times that fit your week.

After each class, the tutor writes notes on what was covered and what to practice next. Notes and homework show up in your dashboard.

## Tracking progress

Students and parents have a dashboard with upcoming classes, past classes and homework. Because every class, note and assignment stays in one place, progress is easy to see.
