# About Learn2Lead

Learn2Lead offers live one-on-one tutoring for K–12 students and test prep: personal tutoring that keeps you a step ahead, and that helps students build skills, confidence and leadership. Learn2Lead is run by Learn2Lead LLC.

Every student gets a dedicated tutor and live online classes. Families buy hours when they need them, and every class, note and assignment stays in one place.

## Who Learn2Lead is for

- Students in grades K–12 who want help with a subject or want to get ahead.
- Students preparing for tests such as the SAT, ACT, AP exams and the LSAT.
- Parents who want to book and pay for tutoring for one or more children.
- Schools and organizations, which can ask about customized plans.
- Tutors who want to teach with Learn2Lead can apply on the website.

## What you get

- **Live one-on-one classes:** video classes with your own tutor, from any phone or laptop.
- **Notes and homework:** after every class your tutor logs what you covered and what to practice next.
- **Reminders and calendar:** email reminders before class, and your schedule in Google, Apple or Outlook Calendar.
- **Message your tutor:** ask questions between classes, right in the app.
- **Pay by the hour:** classes use hours in half-hour steps, and unused hours never expire.
- **Built for families:** one parent account books and pays for every child, with one shared balance.
- **Every class on record:** past classes, notes and homework stay in one place, so progress is easy to see.

Everything for learning is in one place: one app for classes, homework and hours, on the phone you already carry.
