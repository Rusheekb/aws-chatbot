# Learn2Lead FAQ

## What subjects do you offer tutoring for?

Learn2Lead offers tutoring for all K-12 subjects, including Math (from basic arithmetic to calculus), Science (biology, chemistry, physics), English (reading, writing, grammar), History, and test preparation for the SAT, ACT and AP exams. Foreign Languages, Computer Science, the LSAT and Speech & Debate are also offered.

## How are tutoring sessions conducted?

All sessions are conducted online by video, so students can learn from anywhere. Each session is one-on-one with a dedicated tutor who focuses entirely on the student's learning needs.

## How do you match students with tutors?

Learn2Lead pairs each student with a tutor based on their subject, goals, learning style and schedule, and lets you know by email once you are matched.

## What is your pricing structure?

Buy a 4, 8 or 12-hour pack. There is no subscription. Pay once, or turn on auto-refill to buy the same pack again when you run out of hours; you can turn it off anytime in Settings. See the Pricing page for details.

## Do unused hours expire?

No. There's no subscription to cancel. Hours you purchase are yours until you use them, with no expiration date. Need more? You can buy another pack anytime.

## How do I track my child's progress?

Students and parents have a dashboard with upcoming classes, past classes and homework. After each session the tutor writes notes on what was covered and what to practice next.
