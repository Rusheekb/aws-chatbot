# Learn2Lead pricing

Learn2Lead has no subscription. You buy tutoring hours in packs and use them whenever you like. Hours never expire.

Savings are compared with the 4-hour pack's price of $35 per hour.

Every pack includes: use at your own pace, hours that never expire, access to all subjects, and flexible scheduling. Checkout is secure, through Stripe.

## How hours work

You buy tutoring time in hours. Each session uses exactly as many hours as it lasts, deducted in half-hour steps: a 30-minute session uses 0.5 hours, a 1-hour session uses 1 hour, a 90-minute session uses 1.5 hours, and a 2-hour session uses 2 hours.

## Do unused hours expire?

No. Hours carry over indefinitely, with no expiration date. Buy now and use them whenever your schedule allows. There's no subscription to cancel.

## Can I buy more hours later?

Yes. You can top up anytime by buying another pack on the Pricing page.

## One-time or auto-refill

- **One-time:** pay once. Stripe keeps your card so buying more later is quicker; it's charged only when you buy.
- **Auto-refill:** when you run out of hours, the same pack is bought again with the same card, so classes are never interrupted. You can turn it off anytime in Settings.

## Families

A parent buys hours once for the whole family, and every child's classes use the family's shared balance.
